### FR-046 — Attractive Inner Worlds

**Status:** ✅ Implemented

**Problem:** The random zone placement in FR-042 scatters Dwarf and Terrestrial worlds uniformly across all zones. A world with Iron-Silicate composition (ideal for gravity and habitability) could end up in the Outer system, while a Rubble-Pile could land in the Conservative zone by chance.

**Solution:** Added a placement bias inside `placeBodiesV2` Phase C. When the toggle is enabled:
1. Each Dwarf/Terrestrial body's composition quality is scored via `getCompositionQualityRank`.
2. A bias is subtracted from the `3D6` unified zone roll:
   - Rank ≥ 5 (Iron-Silicate, Hydrous, Volatile-Rich): **−2 bias**
   - Rank ≥ 3 (Silicate-Basaltic, Carbonaceous, Silicaceous): **−1 bias**
   - Rank ≤ 2: **no bias**
3. The bias propagates through `rollUnifiedZone` into `rollOuterZone`, so even outer-zone rolls are shifted toward nearer regions (O1–O4).
4. Existing conflict resolution (disk-blocking, Hill-sphere checks, rerolls, ejection) remains unchanged.

**UI toggle:** Added "Attractive Inner worlds" checkbox in the **System Targets** section of the generator dashboard, next to the FDR toggle. Default is **off**.

**Interaction with FR-045 (FDR):**
- Both toggles can be used together. Attractive Inner Worlds shapes the overall distribution during placement; FDR then guarantees the winner is in the Conservative zone if it wasn't already.
